     The size of any such gap cannot be known in advance; even a 15% gap close would cost about $750 + costs, under
     1% of the portfolio.

7. No look-ahead - OK
   - All conditions use Day 0 close data. The Day 1 fill price is unknown by construction and nothing in the plan
     depends on it. No information from after Day 0 is used.

8. Plan agrees with the supplied price data - OK (with the caveat above that I see the Researcher numbers only as

My own analysis of the odds (not a source fact): breakeven win rate after costs = 419.20 / (678.60 + 419.20) = 38.2%.
With no drift, a price path would reach -8% before +14% more often than not (roughly 8/22 = 36% chance of target
first if one of them is reached), so without a real edge this plan is close to or a little below breakeven. The
weak t-stat means the data does not show that edge. This is a reason for caution, not a rule failure.

=== DECISION: APPROVE FOR SIMULATION ONLY ===

Five short points:
1. Every hard limit is met: BUY decided at the Day 0 close, size 5% (limit 10%), stop 8% (limit 15%), target 14%,
   hold 20 days (limit 20).
2. Reward-to-risk after 0.2% per-side costs is 678.60 / 419.20 = 1.62, above the 1.5 minimum.
3. Largest simulated loss is $419.20 (0.42% of the portfolio); even a bad gap through the closes-only stop stays
   under 1%.
4. No look-ahead, and the relayed price facts agree with each other and with the plan.
5. The context is weak (t-stat 0.74, resistance 1% overhead, long-term downtrend, earnings date MISSING), so I expect
   a low hit rate on the target. These risks are real but small at this size, and they do not point to a fixable rule
   failure: any change that would remove them (waiting for a close above 101.00, or a nearer target) would either
   need Day 1 data or push reward-to-risk below 1.5.

What would have to change if the Boss wants more safety: do not increase the size above 5%; if an earnings date
inside the 20-day window becomes known before the Day 0 decision, the plan should become NO TRADE. Nothing else must
change for approval.

Approval is for the simulation only. It does not mean a real trade should be placed. Not investment advice.
