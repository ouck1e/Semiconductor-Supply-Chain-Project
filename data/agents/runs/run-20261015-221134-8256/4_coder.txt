=== FIXED STRATEGY RULES AND LIMITS ===
- Simulated portfolio: $100,000. Long-only: BUY or NO TRADE. No shorting, no leverage.
- The decision is made at the close of Day 0. A BUY fills at the next close (Day 1).
- Maximum position: 10% of the portfolio.
- A stop and a target are required. Maximum stop: 15% below entry.
- Maximum holding period: 20 trading days; the position closes at that day's close.
- Fees 0.1% and slippage 0.1% on each side (entry and exit).
- Stops and targets are checked on daily closes only (there are no intraday highs or lows in the data).
- Simulation only. No real order is ever placed.
